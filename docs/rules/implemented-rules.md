# Implemented rules

What the rules code does today, one bullet per rule, naming where it lives. Moved out of `docs/code-map/README.md` so the map stays a list of files. Numbers come from `state.config` (`shared/src/config.ts`); when each value was confirmed is recorded there and in `docs/rules/rules-design.md`, not here. Open work is in GitHub issues ([project](https://github.com/users/nearbear/projects/1)).

- **Action costs.** Each entry of `PlayerView.actions` carries its `cost`, read through `Action.cost` (the one place costs are read: legality, paying, and the view all go through it; subclasses set the base price in `getCost`). The client displays them and never computes them. Entries you can do now come first with empty `lacks`; then the ones you lack AP or MP for, with `lacks` naming which (`affordable`, `legalIn` in rules.ts).
- **Success chances.** Each `PlayerView.actions` entry's `chance` comes from the action's `chance`; action buttons show the odds in parentheses, e.g. "Punch (60%)", only when below 100%.
- **Action tags** (issue 30, owner 2026-10-18). Each action type declares `tags` (`ActionTag` in `actions/action.ts`), fixed per type like visibility, and each `PlayerView.actions` entry carries them so the UI can mark them (no UI yet). `aggressive`: punch and spike. `spore`: spike (not spore extraction, for balance).
- **Timed actions** (`queue.ts`; `PlayerState.current`, `PlayerState.queue`; `config.action`). Every action but the few marked `instant` (`ready`, `access`, and others) and every terminal action (`terminal = true`; owner, 2026-11-27) takes `action.durationMs` = 2000 ms and happens when its bar runs out (`resolveAt: "end"`; `"start"` makes it happen at once, with the bar as a cooldown). Players act through `queueAction` (via `applyInput`): idle, the action starts; busy, it waits (at most `action.queueCap` = 3, else `queue_full`). The server stamps each action input with `at` and sends a `tick` input at `nextDue`; `tick` resolves due bars earliest first, then in seat order, through `performAction` (checked and paid then; being ready doesn't stop it) and starts the next queued action that is still legal. A dropped action costs nothing and writes a private `actionDropped` line. Each queued entry records `Action.adjustment` (how far modifiers moved its price) when queued; if the adjustment differs when it starts or resolves, it is cancelled with a private `actionCostChanged` line giving the new price, and nothing is paid (owner, 2026-10-18). Base-price changes the queue itself causes (a move's AP-to-MP conversion, a care point used up) don't cancel. The view sends `you.queue` as plain actions and `you.current` without the adjustment. `clearQueue` cancels the queue and, with "end", the action under way. Death clears both. `OtherPlayerView.busy` (times only) goes to people in the actor's room when `activitySeen` (log.ts): public and secret always, covert on camera, private never.
- **Cycles** (#24; `newCycle` in rules.ts, `server/src/cycleClock.ts`). Cycles change on the UTC clock, every `config.cycleHours` (3 by default; a setup may pick 1, 2, 3, 4 or 6, #230) from midnight GMT, so a day starts at 00:00 GMT and has `cyclesPerDay` = 24 / `cycleHours` cycles (`resolveConfig` derives it). Daily effects stay once per 24-hour day; anything counted in cycles stays in cycles. The server sends a `cycle` input at each boundary (one storage alarm for the earliest bar or boundary; after downtime it applies every missed one in time order), and `newCycle` runs `advanceCycle` for everyone, ready or not. A ship launches on the clock's cycle of day 1 (`GameSetup.startCycle`, so 14:10 GMT is Day 1 · Cycle 5 with 3-hour cycles). End cycle (`actions/ready.ts`) is offered only with `config.manualCycles` (false by default; the server sets it from `ALLOW_CHEATS`, and single player turns it on): `ready` marks you done, and End cycle waits on bots only, never on other humans (owner, 2026-11-22): once a living human is ready and every living bot is (or, with no human alive, every living bot), `performAction` calls `advanceCycle` early (`cycleEndsByReady`); goal bots ready once any living human has, so on the dev server the cycle number can run ahead of the clock. `CYCLE_SECONDS` in `server/.dev.vars` shortens cycles for testing, overriding `cycleHours`. The view message carries `nextCycleAt`; the top bar (phone: under the name) counts down to it.
- **Cold disease** (`modifiers/cold_disease.ts`; `modifiers.ts`). Each cycle, a player with `cold_disease` has a 1-in-3 chance to lose 1 MP, floored at 0; the player gets a private log line when it happens.
- **Disease spread in rooms** (`diseases.ts`; owner, 2026-11-20). Driven by each disease's config stats, not its modifier: at each cycle change, each disease a living player carries with a `cycleSpread` chance rolls once per other living player in their room (`spreadAtCycle`, seat order); when someone walks into a room, each disease either side carries with a `roomSpread` chance rolls once to reach the other (`spreadOnEncounter`). Spreaders: Cold, Flu, Rubella, Gastroenteritis and Skin inflammation (the last two spread since 2026-11-20, using numbers already in config). A Mush spreads too.
- **Smallpox** (`modifiers/smallpox_disease.ts`; `config.diseases`; owner, 2026-11-14). Lasts 12–20 cycles. -2 max HP and -2 max morale. At each cycle change, a 40% chance to lose 1 MP (floored at 0) and a 50% chance to lose 2 HP (through `damage`, so it can kill). A Mush carries it without suffering it (`diseaseAffects`). No spread.
- **Rejuvenation** (`modifiers/rejuvenation_disease.ts`; `config.diseases`; owner, 2026-11-14). Lasts 8–12 cycles. At each cycle change, a 50% chance to lose 1 MP (floored at 0), a 10% chance to become dirty (super-dirty: an apron or Stickler doesn't block it), and a 3% chance to catch a disease from `config.diseases.incidentDiseases` the player doesn't have yet (told privately, like the incident). Lying down, a 30% chance at each refill that the bed gives no extra AP (the bed's +1 and Lethargy's bonus; the `onApRefill` hook, `apRefill` in modifiers.ts). A Mush carries it without suffering it. No spread; it joins the fruit disease pool through `allDiseases`.
- **Mush Allergy** (`modifiers/mush_allergy_disease.ts`; `config.diseases`; owner, 2026-11-14). Lasts 20–32 cycles. A human who receives a spore (`contaminate`, through the `onContaminate` hook) dies at once, of `injury` so the public death line doesn't reveal the spore (owner, 2026-11-14). If that spore converts them, they live. Immunity blocks the spore, so it never triggers. A Mush carries it without effect. No spread; it joins the fruit disease pool through `allDiseases`.
- **Acid Reflux** (`modifiers/acid_reflux_disease.ts`; `config.diseases`; owner, 2026-11-15). Lasts 12–20 cycles. After eating food (satiety above 0, so not a drug, as with gastroenteritis), -1 AP, -2 morale, -2 HP (through `damage`, so it can kill) and the player vomits. A Mush loses no stats but still vomits (special logs). No spread; it joins the fruit disease pool through `allDiseases`.
- **Tapeworm** (`modifiers/tapeworm_disease.ts`; `config.diseases`; owner, 2026-11-15). Lasts 20–32 cycles. +2 satiety lost per cycle (3 in all), and hunger starts `hunger.tapewormHungryAfterCycles` (8) cycles after satiety reaches 0 instead of 24 (`isHungry`). Food (satiety above 0) gives half its AP, rounded down, before the AP cap (the `mealAp` fold in consume.ts; drugs are untouched), and the stomach grumbles (`rumble` in diseases.ts, a public log, special logs only). A Mush's numbers are untouched. No spread; it joins the fruit disease pool through `allDiseases`.
- **Pink Eye** (`modifiers/pink_eye_disease.ts`; `config.diseases`; owner, 2026-11-15). Lasts 8–12 cycles. Every shot's success chance ×0.75 (relative): anything tagged `shoot` (turrets), a patrol-ship shot, and a non-melee weapon attack (guns); no `shoot` tag on the latter two, so Gunner's bonus is unchanged. The holder's iTrackie and action buttons are blurred (`blurredVision` in diseases.ts, read from their own view; CSS in `web/src/styles/itrackie.css`). A Mush carries it without effect. No spread; it joins the fruit disease pool through `allDiseases`.
- **Sinus Storm** (`modifiers/sinus_storm_disease.ts`; `config.diseases`; owner, 2026-11-15). Lasts 8–12 cycles. At each cycle change, a 30% chance to lose 1 MP and a 50% chance to lose 1 HP (through `damage`). A 16% chance to sneeze on entering a room (special logs). A patrol ship's shot succeeds ×0.7 (relative). A Mush carries it without effect. No spread; not in the fruit pool.
- **Disorders and Depression** (`modifiers/depression_disorder.ts`, `calmDisorder` in `diseases.ts`, `modifierIds` in `modifiers.ts`; owner, 2026-11-25). A disorder (`config.diseases.allDisorders`) lasts forever (a `forever` tracker). **Depression**: -2 max AP, -2 max morale (floored at 1); no extra daily morale loss (removed, owner 2026-11-26). Nothing to a Mush. Private status (icon `psy_disease.png`). A Shrink's Comfort calms one random still-active disorder of the target for `comfort.disorderPauseCycles` (2–8) cycles (`DiseaseTracker.disabledFor`; nothing if none is active): it does nothing meanwhile, the caps are recomputed when it is calmed and when it acts again, the target gets a private `disorderCalmed` line, and their own status icon is grayed out. Disorders are caught in play through sanity (below). Not built: curing them.
- **Agoraphobia** (`modifiers/agoraphobia_disorder.ts`; owner, 2026-11-26; numbers in `config.agoraphobia`). A disorder (forever; nothing to a Mush or while calmed, as Depression). The holder's room is crowded at 4 or more living crew members (Mush included, the holder included) and hostile NPCs. There, actions that already cost AP cost 1 more AP (free actions and ones paid by skill points stay free; moves aren't charged AP), a move out of the room costs 1 more MP (the room being left counts; a wheelchair helper still makes it free), and each cycle change spent there costs 1 morale with a private `agoraphobiaMoraleLoss` line. Private status (icon `psy_disease.png`).
- **Kleptophobia** (`modifiers/kleptophobia_disorder.ts`; owner, 2026-11-26; numbers in `config.kleptophobia`). A disorder (forever; nothing to a Mush or while calmed, as Depression). Dropping an item costs 2 AP instead of nothing (the `dropAp` fold, part of the drop's base price, so Agoraphobia's and Burnt Hand's +1 AP stack on it: 3 AP in a crowded room); taking stays free. When an expedition's lost-item event destroys an item the holder carries ("stolen"), they lose 4 morale with a private `kleptophobiaMoraleLoss` line (`onItemLost`, run from `expeditions.ts`). Other ways of losing items (a stranded craft, death) don't count. Private status (icon `psy_disease.png`).
- **Addiction** (`sud_disorder`, `modifiers/sud_disorder.ts`; owner, 2026-11-27; numbers in `config.sud`). A disorder in the random disorder pool (forever; nothing to a Mush or while calmed, as Depression). -2 max morale; every action's success chance ×0.75 and its injury and dirt chances ×1.5 (relative, capped at 100%; a sure action stays sure); every morale loss is 1 bigger (the `moraleLoss` fold in `gainMorale`, so the daily loss, a bad drug and Agoraphobia all count; gains don't). Consuming any drug calms it for 3 cycles (`calmDisorderFor`, private `disorderCalmed` log); a drug taken while it is already calmed does nothing. The calm comes before the drug's own effects (the `onDrug` hook in `applyDrug`), so a bad drug's morale loss is not raised.
- **Aquaphobia** (`modifiers/aquaphobia_disorder.ts`; owner, 2026-11-27; numbers in `config.aquaphobia`). A disorder in the random disorder pool (forever; nothing to a Mush or while calmed). A shower costs 2 more AP (after soap's and Water Resistant's discounts) and 3 morale (private log), and only one shower a day is allowed (`daily_cap`): any shower that day counts, even one taken before the disorder or while it was calmed (`PlayerState.showeredToday`, cleared at day start, own view only).
- **Pyromania** (`modifiers/pyromania_disorder.ts`; owner, 2026-11-27; numbers in `config.pyromania`). A disorder in the random disorder pool (forever; nothing to a Mush). -2 max morale; extinguishing (extinguisher or by hand) succeeds ×0.5. At each cycle change a holder who is neither compelled nor in their grace rolls 10% to become compelled for 4 cycle changes (`PlayerState.compelledCycles`, own view; private `pyromaniaCompelled` log). While compelled they get the Pyromaniac's Ignite (secret; the fire catches at the next cycle change) for 2 AP instead of 4. Igniting ends the compulsion; running out costs 6 morale (private log) and ends it. Either way a hidden 2-cycle-change grace follows with no roll (`PlayerState.compulsionGrace`, never sent, not even to the owner). Calmed, everything pauses: no roll, no countdown, no Ignite.
- **Kinesiophobia** (`modifiers/kinesiophobia_disorder.ts`, the `onConvert` hook called from `actions/move.ts`; owner, 2026-11-28; numbers in `config.kinesiophobia`). A disorder in the random disorder pool (forever; nothing to a Mush or while calmed; sanity threshold 95). Each move that converts AP into MP costs 1 morale, with a private `kinesiophobiaMoraleLoss` line; a move paid with MP alone costs nothing extra.
- **Scopophobia** (`modifiers/scopophobia_disorder.ts`; owner, 2026-11-28; numbers in `config.scopophobia`). A disorder in the random disorder pool (forever; nothing to a Mush or while calmed; sanity threshold 100). While a working installed camera or another living player (human or Mush) is in the room, every rolled action's success chance is ×0.9 (relative, like Addiction's); an action that can't fail stays certain. Everyone in the room already sees its cameras and whether they are broken, so the odds reveal nothing.
- **Sanity** (`sanity.ts`, `humanDied` called from `killPlayer` in `death.ts`, `sanityCycle` in `advanceCycle`; owner, 2026-11-25; numbers in `config.sanity`). Every player has a private sanity, starting at 100, floored at 0, kept by the body. When a human dies (not traded, not with the ship), every living player loses 1; living players in the victim's room also lose 4 and are marked as witnesses; whoever dealt the fatal blow (a punch, a weapon attack including its splash, or an interrogation; `damage`'s `killer`) also loses 10 and is marked as the killer, on top of being a witness. A Mush's death costs nothing. At each cycle change every living human rolls once per condition met, each success giving a random disorder they don't hold, with a private `disorderGained` line: witnessed a death since the last cycle change (100 − sanity)%, killed a human since then (100 − sanity) × 2%, morale below 5 (100 − sanity)%. The witness and killer marks then clear. The disorder is drawn only from those whose threshold (`config.sanity.disorderThresholds`, `availableDisorders`; owner, 2026-11-28) is above the player's sanity: Depression, Agoraphobia and Scopophobia 100, Kleptophobia, Aquaphobia and Kinesiophobia 95, Pyromania and Addiction 85; a roll with none eligible gives nothing. Mush never roll. Shown in your own resource chips (icon `psy_disease.png`).
- **Migraine** (`modifiers/migraine_disease.ts`; `relay.ts`; `config.diseases`; owner, 2026-11-15). Lasts 8–12 cycles. A planet analysis reveals 1 sector less (`analysisSectors`). A NERON update and a rebel decode are worth half, rounded down (the `neronProgress` and `rebelGain` folds). Each relay contribution (research, NERON projects, PILGRED) loses `MIGRAINE_RELAY_MALUS` (2) from its base, floored at 0 (`tokenBase`, `relayGainRange`). Lying down, a 30% chance at each refill that the bed gives no extra AP (`withoutBedBonus` in diseases.ts, shared with Rejuvenation). A Mush carries it without effect. No spread; not in the fruit pool.
- **Non-pilot flight** (`patrol.ts`, `actions/takeoff.ts`, `expeditions.ts`; owner, 2026-11-15). Non-pilots can now take off and land patrol ships, the Pasiphae (`takeoff`, `land`, `land_on_planet`), and the Icarus (`icarus_takeoff`) without waiting for the pilot lock. `canFly` drops the `pilot` skill check while keeping the human grounding check for Gastroenteritis and Slight Nausea. Non-pilots roll `cleanManoeuvreChance` (20% clean, 80% rough) on patrol ship/Pasiphae takeoff and landing; if a rough manoeuvre drops ship armor to 0, `destroyPatrolShip` destroys the ship (killing the pilot on takeoff, sparing them in the bay on landing). On expeditions, `LANDING` is only safe with an ungrounded living `pilot` in the crew, or `ICARUS_ANTIGRAV_PROPELLER` on the Icarus (`away.ship === null`; Antigrav propeller does not make patrol-ship planet landings safe).
- **XXL bay doors (`BAY_DOOR_XXL`)** (`modifiers.ts` `cleanManoeuvreChance`, `projects.ts`; owner, 2026-11-15). Once complete, `cleanManoeuvreChance` returns `1` for everyone on patrol ships and the Pasiphae (0% rough manoeuvres on takeoff and landing). Patrol ships and the Pasiphae only (does not affect Icarus expedition `LANDING`). Marked `"full"` in `PROJECT_STATUS`; bay door sprite swap (`bay_window_upgrade`) was already wired in `IsoRoom.tsx`.
- **Slight Nausea** (`modifiers/slight_nausea_disease.ts`; `patrol.ts`; `config.diseases`; owner, 2026-11-15). Lasts 12–20 cycles. A 16% chance to vomit on entering a room; 1 extra satiety lost per cycle; a human can't fly (`canFly`). A Mush carries it without effect. No spread; not in the fruit pool.
- **Food Poisoning** (`modifiers/food_poisoning_disease.ts`; `actions/consume.ts`; `config.diseases`; owner, 2026-11-15). Lasts 8–12 cycles. -3 max HP, -3 max morale; every loss of health is 1 bigger; 1 spore fewer converts; a 16% chance to vomit on entering a room (dirties a crewmate there, `vomit`). A human can't consume anything, food or drug (`cant_eat`). A Mush carries it without effect. No spread; not in the fruit pool.
- **Vitamin Deficiency** (`modifiers/vitamin_deficiency_disease.ts`; `config.diseases`; owner, 2026-11-17). Lasts 12–20 cycles. Every action with a success roll has its chance multiplied by 0.85 (a sure action stays sure). A failed action has a 25% chance to log weakness in the room ("doesn't seem very energetic", `weakness`); no stat change. A Mush carries it without effect. No spread; not in the fruit pool.
- **Flu** (`modifiers/flu_disease.ts`; `config.diseases`; owner, 2026-11-17). Lasts 8–12 cycles. Spreads by STT/DTT, and with a 2% chance to each other living player in the room at a cycle change and on an encounter (as Cold). At each cycle change, a 33% chance to lose 1 MP, a 33% chance to lose 1 health and a 40% chance to get dirty (`soil`, super-dirty: past an apron or Stickler). -3 max HP, -3 max morale. Eating food (not a drug) makes the holder vomit, and so does entering a room 8% of the time (`vomit`). A Mush spreads it and can vomit (special logs) but suffers no stats. Not in the fruit pool.
- **Rubella** (`modifiers/rubella_disease.ts`; `rubellaBlocks` in `diseases.ts`; `config.diseases`; owner, 2026-11-17). Lasts 12–20 cycles. Spreads with a 2% chance to each other living player in the room at a cycle change and on an encounter (as Cold); not by STT/DTT. At each cycle change, a 50% chance to lose 1 MP. -3 max HP, -3 max morale. A 16% chance to scratch on entering a room (`itchy`). Lying down, a 30% chance that the bed gives no extra AP (as Rejuvenation). A human with it can't Cook, Express Cook, Repair, Repair Hull, Repair PILGRED or Renovate (refused `cant_cook`/`cant_repair`). A Mush carries and spreads it without effect. Not in the fruit pool.
- **Syphilis** (`modifiers/syphilis_disease.ts`; `config.diseases`; owner, 2026-11-17). Lasts 20–32 cycles. Spreads by STT/DTT. -3 max HP, -3 max morale; every loss of health is 2 bigger (`damageTaken`); a 16% chance to sneeze on entering a room (`sneeze`). A Mush carries and spreads it without effect. Not in the fruit pool.
- **Single player** (#59; `singlePlayer.ts`, `GameRoom.setupSinglePlayer`; owner, rules-design.md). The lobby's Single player button sends `singlePlayer` to the matchmaker, which launches a new room. `singlePlayerGame` seats the human as `p0`, forced Mush (`mushCount` 1), and 8 `model` bots on characters drawn with the seed from those left, never Eleesha, Chun or Chao (`NEVER_BOTS`). No pre-selection, `manualCycles` on, and no cycle clock: `nextCycleAt` is null and the alarm only settles action bars, so a cycle ends once everyone, bots included, has ended theirs. **Pause (#114):** a model bot with a key but no reply returns null instead of playing scripted; `ModelGate.pause` (`unavailable`, `key`, `budget`) goes out as `botsPaused` in the view message and shows as a top-bar tag (`BotsPausedTag`). `unavailable` retries after `MODEL_RETRY_MS`, doubling to `MODEL_RETRY_MAX_MS`; `key` and `budget` wait. Without a key the bots play scripted (`WanderBot`). No save slots (#243).
- **Movement, AP, HP, morale** (`actions/move.ts`, `moveCost` in `modifiers.ts`). 1 MP per move; with no MP, 1 AP converts to 3 MP; MP starts at 12 (`startMp`, owner 2026-10-24; was 0), cap 12, kept across cycles. AP: start 8, +1 per cycle, cap 12, unspent AP carries over; +2 while lying in a bed once that exists. HP and morale: max 14 each, start at 14 HP and 7 morale, visible only to their owner; every non-zero HP loss or gain (`damage`/`heal` in `death.ts`; owner, 2026-11-22) writes a private `loseHp`/`gainHp` log line (including fatal losses, before death); each living player heals `config.dailyHeal` = 1 HP (up to max, `healDaily` in `death.ts`; owner, 2026-10-28) and loses morale when a day ends (`dailyMoraleLoss` in `modifiers.ts`: `config.moralePerDay` = 2, cut by 1 while Chun lives, see Mankind's Only Hope below; floor 0). Days: `config.cyclesPerDay` = 8; `state.cycle` stays a global counter and `dayOf` derives day and cycle-of-day, both sent in `PlayerView`; the day boundary is handled in `advanceCycle`.
- **Room log** (`log.ts`). Public entries are the room's history, shown to anyone in that room; private entries only ever reach the actor; secret entries become room history if another player witnessed them or a camera is in the room; covert entries become room history only if a camera is in the room (bystanders never see them). A camera is, for now, a `camera` item in the room's inventory (`hasCamera`; one carried by a player doesn't count); a camera reveals the entry to everyone, overriding `hideFrom`. **Window** (owner, 2026-11-05, #365): a seat sees room lines, its private ones included, from the current cycle and the 7 before it (`config.logWindow.cycles` = 8; `seesLine`, `inLogWindow`), 16 with the Tracker skill (`hunt`, picked or from a book; `logWindow.tracker`). Ship-wide lines and chat have no window. **Direction of crew moves and ship logs** (owner, 2026-11-21, 2026-11-22; eMushpedia Traqueur): the state's `left`/`entered` lines (moves and wheelchair pushes) and `patrolTakeoff`/`patrolLanded` lines keep the room/ship, but `viewFor` shows it only to a Tracker (`moveLineFor`): anyone else gets `leftRoom`/`enteredRoom` ("X left the room") or `patrolTakeoffShip`/`patrolLandedShip` ("X took off in a ship" / "X landed a ship") with no room, their own moves/flights included, and a camera adds nothing. Decided as the view is built, so learning Tracker from a book shows it on older lines in the window. A roommate's `spot` at a door (where they came in) also reaches only a Tracker; the client fades the others in and out in place (`doorMoves.ts`, `SceneSprite.fade`). Drones' and Schrödinger's lines keep their direction for everyone. As each cycle begins, `pruneLog` drops room lines older than the longer window, so the state stops growing; the record keeps every line for replays. `PlayerState.logSeen` counts the lines each body could see as they are written (aged-out ones included; a Transfer starts it over for both bodies at what their views show) and goes out as `PlayerView.logSeen`, which the announcer uses to find new lines. **Kind** (owner, 2026-11-05, #366): each line records `LogEntry.kind` (`private`, `secret` or `covert`; none for public), set by the writer rather than the action type (owner, 2026-11-08: a private research's public completion line is public), and `leaked: true` on a secret or covert line that reached anyone besides the actor (a witness, a camera, or an Outcast's room). Every seat sees both on every line it sees, the replay's seat views included. A leaked line is also `level: "warning"`, except an Outcast's, which is only leaked (owner, 2026-11-08). The iTrackie's Room tab shows a badge on each line with a kind (`web/src/hud/VisibilityBadge.tsx`: the action buttons' padlock, hushing hand or camera, with a popover saying what it means and whether it leaked), and colours a leaked covert line red instead of the warning amber.
- **Identity** (owner, 2026-10-17). Players know each other only by character (`Seat.character`; `characterName` in `characters.ts`). Account names (`Seat.name`) stay in server state but are in no view, your own included: `viewFor` strips `name` from `you` (`YouView`) and `OtherPlayerView`, rebuilds chat messages field by field (`ChatMessage` has no name), and log and system-message params carry character ids (`name`, `target`, `body`), which the client's `names.logLine` turns into names. Bots are still tagged AI (`isBot`). Revealing names when the game ends waits on game completion (#191, blocked by #27).
- **Examine** (`actions/examine.ts`, free). On anyone in your room including yourself, the room's equipment, your items, and the room's items; writes a private log line.
- **Inventory.** 3 private slots per player (`config.inventorySlots`, `inventorySlots` modifier hook); rooms hold unlimited items, visible to whoever is there. `take`/`drop` (`actions/transfer.ts`) cost nothing and are logged publicly. On the client, room items that look identical to the viewer (`itemViewKey`, `stackItemViews` in `itemView.ts`) stack into one UI slot with a count badge, while carried inventory stays unstacked; when taking or consuming a room item (`preferSpikedInRoom`), the server picks the highest-spore item among identical-looking visible items in that room (so humans always get a spiked ration from a stacked pile, while Mush — who see `spores` in separate stacks — do not). Items are effect-less placeholders except coffee, food, and the items below. Actions make items in two ways: `produceItem` in `items.ts` hands one to the player (into your inventory, or into the room if it's full, "knocked over"; dispense uses it), and dismantle puts its scraps straight into the room.
- **Dispense** (`actions/dispense.ts`, `dispense.ts`). Equipment can dispense an item (`EquipmentType.dispenses`): free, offered on the refectory's coffee machine; 5% chance to become dirty. Coffee regenerates on a schedule (`GameState.dispensedOn`): base is cycle 1 (once/day); Fission Coffee Roaster adds cycle 5 (twice/day); PILGRED repair adds cycles 3 and 7 (4x/day); both Roaster and PILGRED regenerate every cycle (1..8). The machine holds up to 1 coffee across cycles until taken; taking it empties the machine until the next scheduled regen cycle.
- **Consume** (`actions/consume.ts`, free). An item you carry or one in your room (`ItemType.consume`) is removed and gives AP up to `maxAp`; coffee gives 2 AP and 1 satiety, a `cooked_ration` 4 AP and 4 satiety, and an `uncooked_ration` 4 AP and 4 satiety for 1 morale (owner, 2026-11-05; Sirius adds 1 AP to either; tests in `shared/tests/food.test.ts`). Pools last the target's lifetime.
