# Code map: web

All under `web/src/`. UI rules are in `web/CLAUDE.md`.

## App shell

- `main.tsx`: React entry and client-side routes (React Router, declarative mode, `BrowserRouter`): `/games` the finished games page (lazy), `/editor` and `/design-kit-mush2` dev only (lazy behind `import.meta.env.DEV`), and every other path `App`, which routes `/play` to the game (only with a seat ticket, else back to `/`) and the rest to the lobby; joining pushes `/play`, so Back returns to the lobby with the seat kept. In-app links use React Router's `<Link>` so navigation doesn't reload the page; the Worker's SPA fallback makes a reload on any route work.
- `App.tsx`: the lobby at `/` and the game at `/play` (descendant routes); keeps the seat ticket in sessionStorage.
- `net.ts`: PartySocket connections (host from `VITE_PARTY_HOST`, else the page's own host; in dev Vite proxies `/parties`, `/auth` and `/api` to wrangler, see `vite.config.ts`).
- `lobby/`: the cryopod landing page; a link under the ships goes to `/games`. `Lobby.tsx` + `lobby.css` lay out the panel (DOM); without WebGL2 (`webgl.ts`) it shows a message instead of the ship list, since the game can't be drawn. Signed out, Google and Discord sign-in buttons replace the multiplayer ships (single player stays); a pending sign-up shows the name step instead of the ship list. Aboard a multiplayer ship (`/api/me`'s `aboard`), one Resume button replaces them and skips the ship and character steps (#320); `not_aboard` refreshes `/api/me` and shows the ships with a notice. The pod behind it is a lazy-loaded Pixi canvas (`pixi/PodBackdrop.tsx`, `pixi/PodRenderer.ts`): `podArt.ts` bakes the flat pod with Canvas 2D (behind and in front of the fan), and one fragment shader bends it onto the lid's half-cylinder (`unroll` in `podGeometry.ts`, pure and tested; the GLSL copy must match). Only the fan and stasis light redraw, at most 30 fps. `CharacterPicker.tsx` is the character step: the crew in three difficulty tiers (`difficulty` in `shared/src/characters.ts`, display only), standing sprites on podiums (`design/figure.png`). Desktop: the tiers as rows with a detail pane on the right (portrait, skills with the in-game skill popover, Board button); every crew member's detail text is stacked in one grid cell so the pane never changes height. Phone (`useMobile`): a tab per difficulty, and tapping a crew member opens a modal with their details and the Board button.
- `account/`: sign-in and the account (#286). `api.ts`: the site API through Hono's typed client (`api = hc<ApiType>("/")`, #324; paths, bodies and responses checked against the server's routes), `/api/me` (`useMe`: on a 429 it keeps the last known state, sets `rateLimited` and asks again after `Retry-After`, #305), sign-up, rename, logout, `startSignIn` (a redirect to `/auth/<provider>/start`), and `takeAuthOutcome` (reads and removes `?auth=`). `NameForm.tsx`: the display name field, checked with `checkDisplayName` before sending, used by the lobby's sign-up step and the rename form. `AccountDetails.tsx` + `account.css`: the account's name and rename form (with the warning), the other sign-in method to add, and log out, shared by `AccountButton.tsx` (the lobby toolbar's account button, #66, opening them in a dialog) and `AccountSettings.tsx` (the settings dialog's Account tab in game, #321: loads `/api/me` when opened, says a rename applies from the next ship, and on logout goes back to the main menu; the server revokes the device's seat, the account stays aboard).
- `games/`: the public list of finished games at `/games` (#155), linked under the lobby's ships. `GamesPage.tsx` + `games.css`: `GET /api/games` a page at a time (Newer/Older keep a stack of cursors), each game a card with its end date, cycles, lobby language (named in itself), verdict (`verdictKey` from `EndScreen.tsx`) and crew (ending character, name at the time, alpha/beta Mush or Human by `crewRoleLabel`, skull if dead). Crew in as many columns as fit, one on a phone. Each card links to its replay (`GameCard`'s `replay`, #372). `GameCard` is exported for the replay's too-old summary, without the link.
- `replay/`: the replay page at `/replay/<id>` (#370) and loading a replay (#369). `replayState.ts`: the pure reducer behind `useReplay` (record positions, -1 the launch state; which chunks to fetch, the settled chunk's and the next; keep it and 2 each side; the 200 ms settle; the 409 backoff 1, 2, 4, 8, 15 s then Retry; 429 after `Retry-After`; the history format check; `olderBuild`). `replayViews.ts`: the step's view, `perspective` or `revealFor` through `readFrame`, with all-seeing's streams from the run of loaded chunks ending at the step (entries normalized once each); `readableChunk`, run on the first chunk loaded. `useReplay.ts`: the hook: fetches the index, the summary (`GET /api/games/<id>`) and chunks (aborting unwanted ones), rebuilds each chunk once, computes the view only for the step shown (memoized, through `shareUnchanged`), and `olderBuild` against `__RULES_VERSION__` (stamped by `vite.config.ts`: the commit in a build, "dev" on the dev server). It also returns the loaded `chunks` (read-only), `seats` (`seatsAt` at the step) and `followed` (the seat asked for when it is aboard at the step, else the first aboard; null while nobody is), and `cyclesPerDay`. `transport.ts` (#370): the transport as pure functions: `marksOf` (each loaded chunk's `stepMarks`, cached per chunk), `nextStop`/`prevStop` (raw, skip or event, `EVENT_KINDS`; a chunk not loaded ahead is stopped at its start, so playback loads it), `cycleStart`/`nextCycle`/`prevCycle` (PageUp/PageDown), `dayAt` (the label from the index), `playInterval` (1×/2×/4×), `parseReplayParams`/`replayParams` (`?input&seat&reveal=1`), `transportKey` and `ownsKey` (the keyboard, left alone inside fields, sliders, tabs and scroll areas). `ReplayNotice.tsx` + `replay.css`: loading, preparing, rate limited, not ready or failed with Retry, not found, and too old with the summary card; `OlderBuildBadge`. `ReplayPage.tsx` (lazy route in `main.tsx`): reads `?input&seat&reveal` once and writes the moment back with `replace`; `GameView` without `send`, the clock frozen at the step's time, `nextCycleAt` (the next chunk's `from`) only when `index.realTime`, `snapWalks` on anything but one step forward; playback (a timeout per stop, waiting while `stale`, stopping at the end), the keyboard on `document` (filtered by `ownsKey`); an empty ship's launch state shows the transport and a notice. Back (and the top bar's leave) goes to `/`, not `/games`, when the summary says `singlePlayer`. `Transport.tsx`: the desktop bar (cycle select, cycle scrubber as a Base UI slider over chunks, event and step buttons, play, speed, the skip checkbox, the perspective switcher with its badge, `OlderBuildBadge`, the summary) and the phone strip (step, play, step, the cycle, the switcher, and a "⋯" menu with events, cycles, skip, speed and the summary); a polite status line reads the day and cycle. The stage dims while `stale` (`.game:has(.replay-transport[data-stale])`).
- `games/ShipCard.tsx` + `shipCard.css`: a reusable 200x255 card for one character's finished ship (portrait, triumph score, Mush colour, faded `ENDING_ART` background, end events, highlight icons with counts and tooltips, optional in-app `<Link>` via `to`). Pure props; not used by the game yet, shown on the dev design kit page below. Icons come from `SHIP_CARD_ICONS` in `art/assets.ts`.
- `designKit/Mush2Kit.tsx` + `mush2Kit.css`: the dev-only "Design kit (mush2)" at `/design-kit-mush2` (lazy-loaded in `main.tsx` like `/editor`), showing our own components with sample props, one page per component at `/design-kit-mush2/<id>` with a side nav (a drawer behind a menu button below 1024px). Sections in `CoreSections.tsx`, `HudSections.tsx`, `GameSections.tsx`, `LobbySections.tsx`, `PrimitivesSection.tsx` and `GameScreenSection.tsx`; frames in `KitSection.tsx`; `GameFrame.tsx` is the game screen's layout with skeleton panels, for components that fill a panel. Views are `PlayerView`s built through the rules (`sampleView.ts` helpers, `samples.ts` views; see `docs/plans/design-kit.md`). Linked from the lobby's dev links and the Mush art kit's header; skipped by the hardcoded-text i18n test.
- `WakeUp.tsx` + `wakeUp.css`: the wake-up overlay.
- `GameScreen.tsx`: the live game's socket container: holds the seat's socket and the newest view (plus `cheatsAllowed`, clock offset, next cycle, bot pause) and renders `GameView` with `send`, inside the chat page cache.
- `shareView.ts`: `shareUnchanged`, structural sharing for incoming views: every part equal to the previous view keeps the previous object, so memoized components can skip (web/CLAUDE.md "Render cost").
- `RenderWatch.tsx`: dev builds only, a React `<Profiler>` around `GameView` that warns when one update passes `SLOW_RENDER_MS` and keeps recent update times in `window.__mush2Renders`; production renders the children alone.
- `chatPages.tsx`: the chat page cache (#355): sealed pages fetched with `chatPage`, kept for the game, ticks redrawn from `chatEdit`; `useChatPages` (null without a socket) and `pageItems` for the page bar.
- `GameView.tsx`: the view-only game screen (#333): three panels on desktop, tabbed on phones; renders a `PlayerView` and emits intents through an optional `send`. Without `send` (a replay, #156) it is read-only: it blanks `actions` and `chatChannels` so no action button, Ready, Go or chat input appears, hides Clear queue, private-channel controls, cheats and the body-taken dialog, and floor clicks don't walk; the room list and the iTrackie stay `quiet` (no screen-reader announcements, no focus moves). Replay extras: `clock` (frozen), `transport` (a slot under the top bar on desktop, above the bottom nav on a phone, `.game-transport`), and `snapWalks` (everyone jumps into place, no door walks; `useWalkers`' `snap`). A dead player gets `DeathNotice` instead of the room and no peek bar. Renders the location plate in the room view's top-right corner (`.room-corner`, `styles/iso.css`): one amber chamfered card with your room's name (the `<h2>` that labels the scene, passed to `IsoRoom` as `titleId`), red when your room is on fire, and the minimap (`scene/Minimap.tsx`) under it.
- `Toasts.tsx`: transient messages.
- `chatRead.ts`: chat read state on the client (#400). `createChatReadStore`: one high-water count per channel, raise-only, in localStorage (`chatReadKey`: room plus a hash of the token) and sent to the server throttled (`SEND_EVERY_MS`) and on hide, close and reconnect; `fromServer` takes the max both ways. `ChatReadProvider` (GameScreen), `useReadCount` (one channel), `useReadSnapshot` (all); no provider means everything reads as read. Also "arrived" (`arrivedIn`, `isArrived`, since this client connected; `announce.ts` `freshIn` uses it to decide when to speak, while `unreadToAnnounce` takes the spoken "N new in …" count from the store) and `messageKey`. `hud/chatTabs.ts`: `tabOf`, `unreadByTab` (for the iTrackie tab badges) and `totalUnread` (for the mobile bottom nav's iTrackie badge), `featureOf` and `unreadByFeature` for the feature drop-down. `hud/UnreadChip.tsx`: one channel's unread at the end of its row (threads, private channels, shared planets and reports, features), with the count in the row's accessible name.
- `useMobile.ts`: the phone-layout media query.
- `fogDepth.ts`: the fog depth preference, in localStorage.
- `tips.ts`: the viewer's tip choices in localStorage (on/off, snoozed, muted) and which tip to show (`shownTip`, `forgetEnded`, `dismissed`).
- `rendererChoice.ts`: which renderer draws the room (`ROOM_RENDERER`, Pixi by default; set it to `"dom"` to use the DOM one). No setting or storage.
- `styles.css`: only `@import`s of `styles/*.css`; `responsive.css` must stay last.
- `@mush2/assets/derived/kit.css` (imported by `styles.css`, lives in `Mush/derived/` since it adapts CC BY-NC-SA art): image-free CSS versions of `Mush/www/img/design` pieces (`.kit-progress`, `.kit-tab`, `.kit-minitab`, `.kit-slot`, `.kit-tooltip`, `.kit-popup`, `.kit-box`, `.kit-panel`), any size. The game uses `.kit-slot.big` for empty inventory slots (`hud/InventoryPanel.tsx`); the rest are shown beside the PNG originals on the dev-only design kit page (`web/design-kit.html`, `src/designKit/`).

## hud/

- `TopBar.tsx`: cycle and countdown, Results (once the game has ended), End cycle (only when offered), Leave, settings.
- `CycleCountdown.tsx`: time left until the next cycle, from the view message's `nextCycleAt`; `ClockDigits` draws any h:mm:ss in fixed-width cells (also the expedition return in `ShipStatus`). `useCountdown.ts`: the ticking h:mm:ss behind it and the expedition countdowns. `serverClock.ts`: the server clock as a context (`ServerClock`: live, an offset from ours; in a replay, frozen at the step) and `useServerNow`, read by the countdowns and action bars; `GameView` provides it from its `clock` prop.
- `PlayerSummary.tsx`: the phone top bar's nameplate: portrait (`SelfPortrait`, which opens Inspect on you), name, AP/MP/HP/morale chips, other resources (satiety first).
- `PipBar.tsx`: the pip bar in SVG, sideways or (`vertical`) standing up as the sprite was drawn and filling from the bottom; `height` caps a vertical bar's column in slots, wrapping the rest into side-by-side columns, filling the first column, then the next.
- `apGauge.ts`: `apColumnHeight`, the AP gauge's column height: one column up to 14 slots (Noise Reducer), an even split past it (Lethargy: 2 of 12, or 2 of 13).
- `SettingsDialog.tsx` + `settings.css`: settings dialog (General, Account, and Cheats when allowed).
- `EndScreen.tsx` + `endScreen.css`: the end screen, open while the game has ended until closed (`GameView` holds that; the top bar's Results button reopens it): the ending's banner (`ENDING_ART`), the verdict, and `view.crew` with names, characters, roles and skulls (reuses `settings.css`). Live, a "Watch the replay" link (`replayId`, `ticket.roomId` from `GameScreen`); in a replay it opens at the last step, again after stepping back.
- `BodyTakenDialog.tsx`: the pop-up a Transfer's victim must acknowledge (`you.bodyTakenBy`); only its button closes it, sending `acknowledgeTransfer` (reuses `settings.css`).
- `DocumentDialog.tsx`: `DocumentReader`, the Read button on a document in the Inspect panel and its window listing the rows (Finola's lost analysis, #394); client-only, a `FramedDialog`.
- `Superkube.tsx`: `SuperkubeOpener`, the Superkube's Open button (Inspect and the peek bar, only while cheats are allowed) and its `FramedDialog`: one tab per `KUBE_OPS` group, one row per option, each sending a `kube` input. Lists come from `kubeChoices` (`view.rooms`, `DEFAULT_CONFIG.diseases`).
- `StatInfo.tsx`: the popover over a stat (HP/morale/AP/MP pip bar on desktop, chip on phones) saying how it is commonly spent and regained; the text is `stat.info.*` in the locales.
- `TipCard.tsx` + `tips.css`: one tip at a time, centred above the room view's bottom row on both layouts: ✕ snoozes it until its situation ends, "Don't show this tip again" mutes it; a new tip is announced when announcements are on Everything. The settings dialog has Show tips and Show all tips again.
- `SelfHud.tsx` + `styles/self-hud.css`: your HUD over the room view (desktop only; phones show `PlayerSummary` in the top bar): top left, an ID card on light stock (faction band with `idCard.band.*`, then portrait, labelled name and any held titles (none shown when you hold none), which together are the button that shows you in Inspect; half-size horizontal bars in three sections split by gold dividers (identity top left, action top right: AP, MP and other resources, satiety first, in a row under MP; health across the foot: HP and morale); one cut corner, its fill through `--card-fill`), with the fire notice under it. It is a `.hud-card`, the shared chamfered card in `styles/card.css` (square, bevelled where `--cut-*` says, drawn as clipped layers); the iTrackie and, on the game screen, the panels (inspect, the inventory popup), the top bar and the location plate (amber-outline variant, name and minimap) use the same card.
- `SatietyInfo.tsx`: `useSatietyLines`, what your satiety's card says (cycles with an empty stomach from `satietyZeroSince`, full belly, hungry); satiety is the first chip of your other resources (`ResourceChips.tsx`) on both layouts.
- `Credits.tsx`: the asset attribution and the non-commercial line: a footer at the bottom of the lobby, and in the game at the top of the settings dialog, under its header (both layouts).
- `CommsCenter.tsx`: the Com center terminal window, in tabs Overview (link status and Sol chance, NERON version and update), Xyloph (entries left) and Signals, each with its button when the view offers it. `initialTab` and `initialSignal` (passed on to `Signals`' `initialOpen`) open it on a tab and conversation, for the design kit's Signals section (`signalsSample` in `samples.ts`).
- `Signals.tsx`: the Com center's Signals tab (`docs/plans/signals.md`): every signal in Active and Inactive groups (blue rows: the kind's icon from `SIGNAL_KIND_ICONS` with a tooltip, the name, the caption), each row opening its conversation, and under Active the next rebel base's call (`nextRebel`); without Comms Officer access, one line. `SignalConversation.tsx`: one conversation (name, time left, back button; `AudioWave.tsx`; one feed drawn like global chat with `FeedEntry` and cycle dividers, the signal's lines mirrored to the right; options from `view.actions` under its last line in the same scroll area, each deal one row under one pair of column headings (what you give ⇄ what you get as `ItemFace` tiles with counts, centred under their headings, the skill's icon with its name in a tooltip, dimmed when nobody in the officer's room has it, and a small Deal! button), with dividers between deals; Deal! greyed with the rules' `blocked` reason). A rebel base's conversation has its base art (effect in a tooltip) and one Decode option; its messages are garbled by `garble.ts` (a fixed per-base order, so only clearer as progress rises; whole at 100%). `signalPlayback.ts`: the cosmetic pacing of replies (3 s wait, then typing out; tap to show whole), pure and tested in `tests/hud/signals.test.tsx`.
- `BiosTerminal.tsx`: the BIOS terminal window: each BIOS option with its current value and, for the NERON Admin, a switch button.
- `HunterList.tsx`: the turret window's hunter list (each hunter's hp and a Shoot button, a charges line above), used by `TurretPanel`. `FxSprite.tsx`: one slice of the original's fx sheet at 1x (hunters, the shield and charge icons).
- `TurretPanel.tsx`: a turret's window: its charges and each hunter in range (`HunterList`).
- `PatrolCockpit.tsx`: what the room view shows while you fly a patrol ship (`view.patrolShip`): a space scene (`styles/space.css`) with the ship's docked sprite in the upper right, the hunter you aim at drawn at 2x in a reticle ahead of its nose, the rest of the pack as one sprite with a count in the lower left (press it to aim at the next, shifted right of the AP/MP bars on desktop), and a star field streaming past (`spaceSky.ts`, the iso sky as CSS backgrounds spanning the whole panel). Both your ship and the aimed hunter show their name, a stack of overlapping shield icons (`IconStack`) and their hp underneath the sprite. Ship, target and pack sway on CSS keyframes. The bar has the ship's charges at the top (`IconStack`), one Shoot button flanked by the target-cycling arrow buttons (or arrow keys), and Land and Collect scrap (the options on the ship's command, less Examine; Collect scrap's tooltip gives the debris count, and it shows disabled with the reason when the rules refuse it). The minimap and zoom buttons are hidden, and on phones the ship button stands on the sack. `hunterSlots.ts` (pure, tested) keeps each hunter's number and aim order and counts its hits for the flash. `IsoRoom` hides a ship whose interface is `out`.

**iso/** (details in `web/src/iso/CLAUDE.md`)

- `placement.ts`: pure layout math (projection, paint order, walkable cells, hit-testing), and `doorPassage` (the walk through a door between two rooms, with the doorway's cells marked by the door sprite's key).
- `equipmentTiles.ts`: which tile draws which equipment (`equipmentByTileKey`, also used by the Inspect panel); `withoutBuiltOnly` (drops the map's built-only tiles, the sofas and the lab's MycoScan, at load, remembering each room's by type) and `roomAsBuilt` (adds each back once one of its type is built).
- `scene.ts`: the renderer seam, pure. `describeScene` turns placed sprites into a `Scene` (image frames, positions, animations, effects by meaning, see-through outlines); `SceneRendererProps` is what a renderer takes. Also `Occupant`, `Corpse`, `imageOf` (a sprite's frame, for hit-testing), `animStep` (per-frame rhythms, holding the last frame) and the door animations (`doorAnim`, `doorOpenMs`).
- `pixi/PixiRenderer.ts` + `pixi/PixiScene.tsx`: the PixiJS renderer (#194), the default (`rendererChoice.ts`), lazy-loaded. Imperative: one Pixi sprite per scene sprite, kept by key; renders on demand; fog as tint; redraws after a lost WebGL context; marks its host `data-art="loading"|"ready"` for the screenshot tools. `pixi/effects.ts` (pure) turns each effect and shadow into numbers matching `styles/iso.css`; `pixi/filters.ts` has the hand-written WebGL filters (shadow, brightness and glow in one pass; the see-through outline in two), shared between sprites with the same look. `pixi/fire.ts` draws a burning room's fire (after the original's `CellFire`): spread over the floor by seeded value noise (`fireWeights`), the original's amount (`emitterCount`), one layer per floor cell that the renderer puts at the cell's place in the paint order (`Scene.fire`, from `placeWithSlots` in `placement.ts`), particles from `FX_FIRE_X`/`FX_FIRE_X2` aging through yellow, orange, red and smoke. Sprites with `bob` (a support drone, #411) float up and down 2px, keeping it rendering while one shows. Biome keeps `pixi.js` imports inside `pixi/`.
- `starfield.ts` (pure): the procedural star field behind the ship, in the colors of the original backdrop `Mush/clients/iso/gfx/bg.png` (seeded, seamless 1024 px tile). The Pixi renderer draws it as a `TilingSprite` under the scene, drifting at half the camera's speed and turning a quarter the other way when the ship turns (`Scene.turn`; the math is `skyTurn.ts`, pure and tested); the DOM renderer has no sky.
- `dom/DomScene.tsx`: the DOM renderer (one div per sprite, `fx-*` effect classes, the see-through SVG filter, `useAnimFrame`).
- `SceneOverlay.tsx`: status badges, action bars and speech bubbles over people, DOM whatever the renderer.
- `useDomFrame.ts`: per-frame camera transform and walker shifts for a DOM layer (DomScene and the overlay).
- `IsoRoom.tsx`: the controller: places the scene, follows you with the camera, hit-tests and handles input, and draws through `Renderer` (DomScene); the layout holds a `camera`/`camera2` tile in every room, drawn only in your own room and only when `view.roomEquipment` has a `CAMERA` (and clickable as that equipment); handles clicks. Corpses on the floor are placed as actors and drawn with the lying pose (`lyingFrame`); clicking one selects the item. Clicking a chair sits you on it (`seatFor`, `chairAt`, `sittingFrame` in `art/assets.ts`). Floor and chair clicks, and walks up to the target of a public action, go to the server (`onWalk`, a `walk` message); others stand at their view `spot` (a cell, in front of the door they came in by, or at a terminal: `terminalSpot` in `placement.ts`) and sit once they reach the chair. With a terminal's window open (`atTerminal`) you go to the chair the server gave you; once it closes you walk to the terminal's standing spot, which sends the `walk` that ends it (#192). In dev builds it puts an `isoProbe` on `.iso-viewport` (clickable things with their screen boxes, the hover key) so the screenshot tools work whichever renderer draws.
- `useWalkers.ts`: walking (drawing only; where people head comes from `IsoRoom`): everyone in your room walks to their spot along `walkPath`, moved per frame by whole pixels (handed to the renderer through a `paint` callback) with the camera locked on you; facing by screen direction. A `Goal` can come in (`from`) or go out (`then`) through a door; `stepWalkers` (pure, one frame) opens doors ahead of whoever is coming through and shuts them behind. Nothing waits for a walk.
- `doorMoves.ts`: `recentDoorMoves`, who just came in or went out and by which door, from the room log's `entered`/`left` entries; others walk through that door.
- `LeavingRoom.tsx`: `LeavingIsoRoom`, what `GameView` renders: `IsoRoom`, but on a room change it keeps the room you left on screen until you have walked into its doorway.
- `useActingOn.ts`: what your latest action is on (or the door, for a move), read from the view's queue (`begun`), so you walk up to it when it starts; `IsoRoom` turns it into a cell.
- `hitMask.ts`: per-frame alpha masks for hit-testing.
- `shipLayout.ts`: fetches the layout JSON and maps atlas sheets to image URLs. `useCheatRoom` adds the cheat room (#170, `Mush/derived/cheat-room.json`, a dynamic `import()` fetched only once you are in it) and the Superkube's sprite (`addCheatRoom`; the owner's `Mush/derived/items/super_kube.jpg`).
